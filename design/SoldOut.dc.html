The treatment

Photo

grayscale(1) at opacity 0.5. Desaturating rather than hiding is what keeps the dish recognisable — the diner still sees what they are missing.

Ink

Name #9A949A, description and price #A8A2A8, veg mark #B4AFB4. The price stays visible and is NOT struck through — it is not a discount, and a strike would read as one.

Tag

Manrope 800 · 10.5px · 0.06em tracking, #6B646B on #EFEDEF, radius 4px. Sits where the badge sits on an available card, so the row rhythm does not shift. Grey, not red: nothing has gone wrong.

Behaviour

Not tappable — no detail sheet, no ADD button, aria-disabled and the label reads "Ghee Roast, sold out". Sorted to the bottom of its own category, keeping its relative order among the other sold-out items.

A category where every item is sold out still renders its header. If that reads badly on a small menu, the alternative is to hide the whole category — worth deciding once real shops have toggled things off.
