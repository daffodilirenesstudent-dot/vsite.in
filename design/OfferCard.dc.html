What changes from a normal card

Price row

Manrope 800 · 19px · #191919 for the live price, replacing the normal card's Poppins 600 · 16px · #EF59A1. Struck original Manrope 500 · 12px · #918992. Saving Manrope 800 · 11.5px · #13801C, as plain text — the green chip is gone.

Photo flag

Anchored bottom-left of the 108px thumb, radius 0 6px 0 10px so its outer corner follows the thumb's own 10px. Solid #EF59A1, Manrope 800 · 10px. Percent only — the rupee saving is already in the price row.

Everything else

Unchanged. Ground #FAFAFA, border 1px #E6E6E6, radius 8px, no shadow — so an offer card and a normal card sit at the same visual weight in a scrolling list.

One decision to confirm: the live price now uses dark ink (#191919) rather than the brand pink. That is what makes the struck-through original and the green saving readable beside it — but it means a discounted dish shows a dark price while every undiscounted dish shows a pink one. The alternative is #C42A73 (deep pink), which keeps price colour consistent at some cost to contrast.
