Detail sheet — read, not order

vsite takes no orders, so the sheet is a reference page. Everything that implied a transaction is gone.

Removed

Quantity stepper. Counting portions you cannot submit.

Total price block. A running total is a basket by another name.

Selectable size pills. The real damage: they look like a choice, respond to a tap, and lead nowhere. A diner who picks "Family" reasonably expects something to happen.

In its place

Sizes as a price list. Rows with a hairline rule — the same information, formatted as a menu rather than a form. Nothing looks pressable.

Combos list their contents. "What's inside" is the question a combo actually raises, and it was never answered anywhere.

One honest closing line. "To order, please tell our staff" — the sheet ends by telling the diner what to do next instead of dead-ending.

Motion, unchanged

Backdrop 0→200ms · sheet 0→450ms on cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1) · photo scale(1.06→1) over 700ms · content staggered +60ms · close button last. Transform and opacity only; under prefers-reduced-motion everything resolves to its end state.

The closing line is new copy I added rather than found — say if you want different wording, a Tamil version beside it, or it gone entirely.
